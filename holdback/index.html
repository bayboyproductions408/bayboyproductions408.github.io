Holdback: Freelance Tax

Works out what to set aside from each payment, so tax season is not a surprise.

Holdback is for self-employed and freelance income. Enter what you were paid and it tells you how much of it is not really yours, so the money is already waiting when the bill arrives.

It is a planning aid, not tax advice, and not a substitute for an accountant.
