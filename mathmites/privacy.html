Mathmites · Math Games for Kids

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Mathmites collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet connection. Everything the app learns about your child stays on your device, and we never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Not a name, not an email address, not a device identifier, not an advertising ID, not location, not contacts, not photos, not usage analytics.

The app asks how old the player is on first launch. That answer is stored on the device and used to pick a starting difficulty. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is not attached to any identifier.

What is stored on your device

Your child's learning progress: which math skills they have practised, how many questions they answered, which Mathmites they have collected, and their settings. This lives in the app's own storage on the device.

It is not backed up to our servers, because we do not have servers. Deleting the app deletes all of it, permanently.

Children's privacy

Mathmites is designed for children roughly four to nine years old, and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy.

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for a parent to request, review, or ask us to delete. We could not identify a particular child's data if we wanted to.

Permissions

Mathmites requests no device permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library, or Bluetooth, and it does not need network access to work.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that changes this — optional advertising or a paid subscription, for example — this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Any such change will be described here plainly rather than buried, and the app's store listing will say so too.
