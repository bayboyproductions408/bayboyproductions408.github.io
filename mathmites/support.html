Common questions

How do I get to the parents area?

Tap Parents at the bottom of the home screen. It is behind a short multiplication question, so a young child cannot wander into it. Inside you will find a progress report showing which topics are solid and which need practice, plus the calm mode setting.

What is calm mode?

Calm mode turns off all movement in the games. Nothing drifts, nothing approaches, and there is no time pressure at all. It is meant for children who find timed drills stressful, and it can be switched on in the parents area.

My child is getting questions that are too hard or too easy.

The app adapts on its own. It tracks each of the 114 skills separately and keeps returning to whatever is shaky, using spaced repetition, so it settles into the right level within a few sessions. You can also change the starting age in the parents area, which moves the whole starting point.

Is there a way to lose, or to run out of turns?

No. There are no lives, no energy meters, no timers you can fail, and no way to get a game over. A wrong answer shows the right one and the game moves on. Flashcards is the one exception: that card comes back until the answer is typed, and it shows the answer after two tries so nobody gets stuck.

Does the app collect any information about my child?

No. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access. Progress is stored only on your own device. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I delete my child's data?

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it, permanently. There is nothing on a server anywhere, because there is no server.

I found a wrong answer in a question.

Please tell us, and include the exact question if you can. Every generated question is checked automatically against an independently recomputed answer, but if something has slipped through we want to fix it quickly. A math app being wrong is the worst bug it can have.
