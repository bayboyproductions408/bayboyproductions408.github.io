Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. It is short, because the app does very little.

Last updated 24 August 2026

The short version

Accounts
There are none. No login, no sign-up, no email address.
Your data
We collect nothing. Your progress is saved on your device only.
Ads
Served by Google AdMob, and requested as non-personalized.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing.

Golf Putt Putt has no account, no login, no sign-up form and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device only:

  • which holes you have completed, and your best stroke count on each
  • coins, mulligans, and which ball skins you have unlocked
  • your sound on/off setting
  • a randomly generated local ID, used only to name your save file

This never leaves your device, and it is not backed up to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Golf Putt Putt shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google and is a separate company from us.

The app is configured to request non-personalized ads. That means ads are selected based on general context rather than a profile of you. Google may still process limited data — such as your device’s IP address and general device information — in order to serve an ad, prevent fraud, and count impressions. We never see this data and never receive it.

Google’s own policy explains what they do with it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

Golf Putt Putt is rated 4+ / Everyone and is suitable for all ages. It is a general-audience game, not a child-directed one under COPPA. Ad content is restricted to a G content rating.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect personal information at all.

In-app purchases

There are none in version 1.0. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

Third parties

The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, described above. On devices where Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) is available, leaderboard scores you submit are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Leaderboards are optional — the game is fully playable without signing in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it, and the updated policy will be published at this same address before the change takes effect.

Support

One address handles everything — questions about this policy, bug reports, a hole that will not complete, lost progress, or anything else about the game. Emails are usually answered within two business days.

If you are reporting a problem, it helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and the number of the hole you were playing.
