Common questions

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen and the bird follows your finger anywhere you want to go — it is not a flap against gravity, you have the whole screen. You fire forward automatically, so there is no shoot button to hold. Dodge or destroy the drones and asteroids, fly through the gaps in the barriers, and reach the level's target score to clear it.

What are the abilities, and how do I get them?

Five of them drift through every level as glowing pickups, and you collect one by flying into it. Blast shatters the barrier in front of you, Rewind pulls the last three seconds back, Shield absorbs one hit, Invincibility makes you untouchable for a few seconds, and Dash punches you through something you were about to clip. You start every run with nothing, so what you have at the end is whatever you caught along the way.

I died near the end of a level and got sent back to the middle, not the start.

That is a checkpoint, and it is deliberate. Get far enough into a level and it saves your position, so a death drops you back into the middle of the fight rather than the beginning. Finishing a level and choosing Play Again starts a fresh attempt from zero — the checkpoint is specifically a death-recovery mechanic.

A level is locked.

Levels unlock in order — clear the one before it. Your progress and best score per level are shown on the campaign screen.

What is Classic Mode?

A simpler one-tap survival mode, reachable from the button at the bottom of the campaign screen. Tap to flap, fly as far as you can. It shares your coins and your whole bird collection with the campaign.

How do I unlock birds and accessories?

Coins are earned by playing — collected mid-flight and awarded for destroying drones and asteroids. Spend them in the Bird Shop, which holds 105 bird colours and 70 hats, glasses and neckwear. A few of the birds change how you fly (Coin Magnet, Guardian Shield, Featherfall); the rest are purely cosmetic. Nothing in the shop can be bought with real money.

The Leaderboard button does nothing.

The leaderboard needs Game Center on iOS, or Google Play Games on Android, and it stays unavailable until you are signed in at the operating-system level — on iOS that is Settings, then Game Center. Signing in is entirely optional and the rest of the game works exactly the same without it.

Does the app collect any information about me?

Nothing of its own. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics and no account. Your save file lives only on your device. The one exception is optional: if you sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games, your best score and total coins are sent there so they can appear on the leaderboard. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app — the local save goes with it. Leaderboard entries are held by Apple or Google under your player account, and are removed through Game Center or Google Play Games directly.
