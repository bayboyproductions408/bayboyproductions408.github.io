Eat Fast Move · Meals, Fasting & Workouts

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

Eat Fast Move stores everything you enter — meals, weight, goals, workouts, fasting sessions — only on your own device. There's no account, no login, and no server of ours your personal data is uploaded to. We don't sell your data or run any analytics or tracking SDK beyond the ad SDK described below.

What leaves your device, and why

Three things, each for one specific reason:

WhatSent toWhy
A meal photo, when you scan oneGoogle Gemini APIIdentify the food and estimate nutrition
Food or restaurant search text you typeOpen Food FactsLook up nutrition facts for that item
Device advertising identifierGoogle AdMobRequest an ad, and personalize it if you allow tracking

None of these include your name, profile, meals, weight, goals, or any other data you've entered elsewhere in the app — each request carries only what it needs to answer that one request.

What stays on your device

Your profile, meal logs, weight history, fasting sessions, and workout plans and logged sets all live in the app's own local storage. None of it is backed up to a server we run, because we don't run one. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it — there's no server copy to separately request deletion of.

Third-party services this app calls

  • Google Gemini API (photo → food identification). Google's own terms govern how they handle that image; on the free tier we currently use, Google may use submitted content to improve their products. See Google's Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) — a free, open database used to look up nutrition data for packaged and branded foods you search for. Only the search text you type is sent. See Open Food Facts' privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob — displays the ads that keep the app free. See Advertising below.

Advertising

Eat Fast Move is free to download and use, and pays for that through ads shown on a few screens (Calendar, Profile, Workouts) plus, optionally, a short video ad you can choose to watch to unlock extra content — never a required ad blocking something you already had access to. Ads are never shown on the camera/scan screen, the meal-review screen right after a scan, or the Fasting timer.

Ads are served by Google's AdMob, which uses a device advertising identifier (not your name or account — there is no account) to request ads and, if you allow it, show ones more relevant to you. On iOS, you'll see Apple's standard "Allow Tracking" prompt the first time you open the app — if you decline, the app still works exactly the same and still shows ads, just without personalization. See Google's AdMob privacy policy and how Google uses data from advertising partners.

Permissions this app asks for, and why

  • Camera — to take a photo of your food for the scan-a-meal feature.
  • Photo library — only if you choose "pick from library" instead of taking a new photo.

No location, contacts, microphone, or any other device permission is requested.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective date" above will be updated and the new version posted at the same location. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Any such change will be described here plainly rather than buried, and the app's store listing will say so too.
