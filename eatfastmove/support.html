Common questions

Do I need an account to use the app?
No. There's no sign-up, no login. Everything you log — meals, weight, workouts, fasts — stays on your own device.
How do I delete my data?
Uninstall the app. Since nothing is stored on a server, that removes everything — there's no separate account to close.
Why does scanning a photo need an internet connection?
The photo is sent once to an AI vision service to identify what's on your plate, then the result comes right back — that part can't happen fully offline.
The calorie estimate looks off for something I scanned. What do I do?
Photo scanning is a fast estimate, not a lab measurement — tap any item on the review screen to edit it by hand before saving. For restaurant items, search the restaurant directly for the official nutrition facts instead of scanning.
Does the fasting timer work if I close the app?
Yes. Your fast is timed from when you started it, not while the app is open — close it, lock your phone, come back whenever, and the timer and milestone ring will have kept going.
Can I use it without ads?
The app is free and ad-supported. Ads only appear on browse screens (Calendar, Profile, Workouts) — never on the camera/scan flow, the meal-review screen, or the Fasting timer.
